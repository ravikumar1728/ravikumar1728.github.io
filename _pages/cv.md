### Research Experience

**Dynamics of Open-Quantum Systems** | *Aug 2021 – Nov 2022*
*Advisor: Dr. Rajeev Singh, Indian Institute of Technology, Varanasi*
* Studied Open-Quantum Systems, which are strongly coupled with the environment, and their dynamics.
* Implemented the non-Markovian dynamics of an unbiased spin-boson model using the time-evolving matrix product operator (TEMPO) algorithm.

**Quantum Quench in Spin-Chains** | *June 2020 – July 2021*
*Advisor: Dr. Rajeev Singh, Indian Institute of Technology, Varanasi*
* Studied quantum quench in a Heisenberg spin-chain and carried out computations using state-of-the-art tensor network techniques like the Density Matrix Renormalization Group (DMRG) and Time-Evolving Block Decimation (TEBD). 
* Worked out the spin, energy transport, and entanglement dynamics for these systems.

**Photon Transport Through 1-D Open Quantum System** | *Jan 2020 – May 2020*
*Advisor: Dr. Rajeev Singh, Indian Institute of Technology, Varanasi*
* Studied photon transport through an atomic medium modeled by a one-dimensional interacting spin chain coupled to photon baths at the boundaries. 
* Used the quantum Langevin equation (QLE) to work out the transient and steady-state transport properties of a monochromatic laser light passing through the atomic medium.

**Quantum Phase Transition in Sawtooth Spin-Chain Systems** | *May 2022 – July 2022*
*Advisor: Dr. Subhro Bhattacharjee, International Centre for Theoretical Sciences, Bengaluru*
* Investigated a spin-1/2 sawtooth chain with competing Heisenberg antiferromagnetic interactions between their nearest neighbors. 
* Utilized the DMRG technique to characterize the ground-state phase diagram in the spin-1/2 sawtooth chain.

**Quantum Phase Transition in Sawtooth Spin-Chain Systems** | *May 2021 – Jan 2022*
*Advisor: Dr. Ranjan Modak, Indian Institute of Technology, Tirupati*
* Studied a geometric quench on a system of fermions in a one-dimensional lattice in the presence of a quasiperiodic potential using the DMRG and TEBD techniques. 
* Used features like site occupation and the spreading of entanglement with time to characterize the localization-delocalization transition.
